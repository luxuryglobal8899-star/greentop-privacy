Greentop ERP Privacy Policy

Greentop ERP is an internal business application used to manage our eBay selling activities.

The application may access eBay seller account information, order information, listing information, and selling analytics through the eBay APIs.

This information is used only for internal business operations and is not sold or shared with third parties.

Access credentials and eBay authorization tokens are kept private and are used only to communicate with eBay APIs.

For privacy questions, please contact Greentop Tech.
